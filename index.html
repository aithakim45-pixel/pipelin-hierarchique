What is "Realistic Coins/Hr"?

Unlike raw profit margin, this model calculates true hourly throughput by measuring weekly instant-buys and instant-sells, active order undercutting, and your realistic market share. No fake millions from dead items!

Anti-Manipulation Protection

When market manipulators place a fake order of 1 item to inflate the apparent spread, SkyFlip compares depth and order quantity to flag traps with ⚠️ TRAP badges.
