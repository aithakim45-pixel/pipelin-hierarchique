How The Forge Works

The Forge allows you to craft high-tier gear and refined materials passively. With 5 unlocked Forge slots and the Quick Forge perk, you can generate tens of millions in passive profit per day!

Profit / Hour Metric

Always prioritize Profit / Hour rather than total profit! Forging an item with +2M profit in 4h gives you 500k/hr, which beats an item with +4M profit that takes 24 hours (166k/hr).
